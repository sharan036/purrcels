How to play

Three rules. Five cats.

Each row, column, and colored patch needs exactly one cat. Start with the patch that has the fewest possible spots.

A

One cat per patch

Patch letters match the colors, so you can follow the regions without relying on color alone.

↔

One per row and column

Once a cat has a spot, rule out the other tiles in its row and column.

◇

No touching

Cats need a little breathing room. A shared side or corner is off-limits.

A little help

How do I mark an impossible tile?

Select Mark, then choose the tile. Choose that tile again to clear the mark. Marks are just notes.

What does Hint do?

It reveals one correct spot and explains what to do next. If a cat is in a spot that can’t be part of the solution, the hint points it out first.

Will my progress be saved?

This web demo does not save your board. It resets if you reload or leave the page.
