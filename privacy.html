Purrcels website & mobile app

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers both the Purrcels website (https://sharan036.github.io/purrcels) and the Purrcels: Cat Logic Puzzle Android mobile app (package: com.purrcels).

Playing on this site

The browser puzzle runs on your device. We do not ask you to sign up or enter personal information to play. Your moves stay in the open page and are reset on reload. The browser puzzle keeps moves in memory.

Studio dashboard

Studio is available to authorized game operators. The game server verifies an operator’s username and password and sets a secure sign-in cookie that expires after eight hours. It stores a password hash and a hash of the session token. The dashboard reads pseudonymous ranked attempts and aggregate counts from the game database. It stores each saved review with its note, operator, revision and time in that database. A CSV download places the selected results and their notes on the operator’s device.

Mobile app: Offline mode & local storage

You can play Purrcels: Cat Logic Puzzle offline without signing in. When playing in offline or guest mode, your puzzle progress, preferences, and unlocked helpers are stored only locally on your device and are not sent to external servers.

Google Play Games & OAuth data usage

The Android mobile app offers optional sign-in through Google Play Games Services so you can back up and sync your progress across devices. If you choose to connect Google Play Games, the app accesses the following information via Google OAuth / Play Games APIs:

  • Data accessed: Your Google Play Games Player ID, gamer tag (display name), profile avatar, and game progress / cloud save data (such as solved puzzles, streaks, and unlocked helpers).
  • Purpose of use: This data is used strictly to authenticate your player profile, display your gamer tag and avatar inside the app, and sync or restore your game progress across devices via Google Play Games Saved Games / Cloud Sync.
  • Limited use & sharing: Purrcels does not sell your user data, does not use Google user data for advertising or marketing, and does not share Google OAuth or Play Games data with third parties other than Google Play Services to provide authentication and cloud save functionality.

Website hosting

If you access this site through GitHub Pages, your browser connects to GitHub to load the pages and images. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to provide and secure its services. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information on GitHub’s practices, retention, and privacy contacts.

Other sites

Links to other websites or services (such as Google Play) are governed by those services’ own privacy policies. Neither this website nor the mobile app embeds third-party ads or behavioral trackers.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your game data at any time using any of the following options:

  1. Disconnect Google Play Games: Revoke app access in your Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or manage/delete saved game data in your Google Play Games settings.
  2. Delete local app data: Clear the app’s storage in your Android device settings or uninstall Purrcels: Cat Logic Puzzle to remove all locally stored progress.
  3. Contact us by email: Reach out to the developer at the email below to request deletion of any associated records.

On the website, reloading or closing the page clears your current board. GitHub controls retention of information that it collects as the hosting provider.

Updates to this policy

We will update this page if our website or mobile app data practices change, and revise the "Last updated" date above.
