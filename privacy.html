Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Purrcels: Cat Logic Puzzle (Android package: com.purrcels) and the Purrcels website (https://sharan036.github.io/purrcels) handle your information. This policy covers both the browser puzzle on our website and the Purrcels: Cat Logic Puzzle Android mobile application.

Playing on the website

The browser puzzle runs directly on your device. We do not ask you to sign up or enter personal information to play on the web. Your moves stay in the open page and reset on reload. The website’s code does not send your moves to a server or use cookies, local storage, advertising, or analytics scripts.

Mobile app: Offline mode & local storage

You can play Purrcels: Cat Logic Puzzle on Android completely offline without signing in. When playing in offline or guest mode, your puzzle progress, preferences, and unlocked items are stored only locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

Google Play Games & OAuth data usage

The Android mobile app offers optional integration with Google Play Games Services so you can sign in and back up your progress. If you choose to connect your Google Play Games account, the app accesses the following information via Google OAuth / Play Games APIs:

  • Data accessed: Your Google Play Games Player ID, gamer tag (display name), profile avatar, and game progress / cloud save data (such as solved puzzles, daily streaks, and unlocked helpers).
  • Purpose of use: This data is used strictly to authenticate your player profile inside the app, display your gamer tag and avatar in-game, and sync or restore your game progress across devices using Google Play Games Saved Games (Cloud Sync).
  • Limited use & sharing: We do not sell your personal or Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes, and we do not share Google OAuth or Play Games data with any third parties other than Google Play Services as required to provide authentication and cloud save functionality.

Website hosting

When you visit our website on GitHub Pages, your browser connects to GitHub to load the pages and assets. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to provide and secure its hosting services. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details on GitHub’s practices and retention.

Other sites and third-party services

Links to external websites or services (such as the Google Play Store or GitHub) are governed by their respective privacy policies. Neither our website nor our mobile app embeds third-party advertising networks or behavioral trackers.

Data retention and deletion

You remain in control of your game data and can delete it at any time using any of the following methods:

  1. Revoke Google Play Games / OAuth access: Disconnect Purrcels from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions or manage/delete your cloud save data inside the Google Play Games app settings.
  2. Delete local app data: Clear the app’s storage in your Android device settings or uninstall Purrcels: Cat Logic Puzzle to permanently remove all locally stored progress.
  3. Request deletion by email: Contact us at the email address below to request assistance or deletion of any associated records.

On the website, reloading or closing the page immediately clears your current board.
