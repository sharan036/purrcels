Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Purrcels website and its browser puzzle. It does not describe the data practices of any separate mobile app.

Playing on this site

The browser puzzle runs on your device. We do not ask you to sign up or enter personal information to play. Your moves stay in the open page and are reset on reload. This site’s code does not send your moves to a server or use cookies, local storage, advertising, or analytics scripts.

Website hosting

If you access this site through GitHub Pages, your browser connects to GitHub to load the pages and images. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to provide and secure its services. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for information on GitHub’s practices, retention, and privacy contacts.

Other sites

Links to other websites are governed by those websites’ own privacy policies. This website does not embed third-party ads, trackers, or widgets.

Retention and deletion

The site does not retain your puzzle progress. Reloading or closing the page clears your current board. GitHub controls retention of information that it collects as the hosting provider.

Updates to this policy

We will update this page if the website’s data practices change.
